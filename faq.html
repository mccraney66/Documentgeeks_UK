Frequently Asked Questions

Questions are welcome.

If you have a question about a service, deadline, special request, or technical issue, contact us. We are happy to help.

What kinds of subpoenas or legal documents can you serve?

If your documents are prepared for service, contact us with the service location, deadline and details. We will review the request and confirm whether we can handle it.

I do not have a service contract. Can you still offer tech support?

Yes. A tiered support agreement can reduce ongoing support costs, but a contract is not required to ask for technical help.

Can you do a rush order?

In many cases, yes. Rush availability depends on the service, location, timing and workload. Call the office for current availability.

What kind of courier services do you offer?

We can move documents, files and some small items between offices. For larger or recurring time-sensitive routes, contact us so we can confirm whether we can accommodate the schedule.

I have a small or large office. Can you still help digitize my records?

Big or small, we love them all. We will build a plan around the size of the project so records are copied and returned in a practical timeframe.

Can I receive updates by text message?

Yes, when SMS is offered for your service. Text messaging is optional and uses a separate opt-in. You can reply STOP to opt out at any time.
