Digital Archival

Are you drowning in paperwork?

We can help turn closed cases and paper files into accessible digital records while freeing valuable office space.

Make records easier to find and easier to keep.

Document Geeks can digitize files and closed cases, then deliver the resulting records in the format and storage method that fits your operation. Depending on your needs, records can be delivered on removable media, stored on a secure server at your location, placed in cloud storage, or made available through a hosted solution arranged with Document Geeks.

For organizations receiving a high volume of subpoenas, we can also discuss records-handling support and custodian-of-records assistance based on your organization’s needs.

Big office or small office: we build the project around your records, your timeline and how your team needs to access the finished files.
