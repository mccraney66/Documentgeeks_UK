Text Messaging

SMS Terms

SMS participation is optional and uses an unchecked opt-in on the contact form.

Program description

When you opt in, Document Geeks may send service-related text messages such as request confirmations, scheduling information, project or order updates, reminders, support communication and follow-up related to your interaction with us.

Message frequency and charges

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply depending on your mobile carrier and plan.

Opt out and help

Reply STOP to opt out at any time. We will honor applicable opt-out requests and may send one final confirmation that you have been unsubscribed. Reply HELP for assistance. You can also contact Document Geeks at (951) 923-2527 or Info@DocumentGeeks.com.

Consent

Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of purchase. Provide a mobile number only if you are authorized to use it.

Privacy and mobile information

Mobile information, SMS opt-in data, and SMS consent will not be sold, rented, or shared with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing or promotional purposes. Information may be provided to telecommunications carriers, messaging aggregators, platform providers, and service providers only as reasonably necessary to deliver and support the messaging service. See the Privacy Policy for complete details.
