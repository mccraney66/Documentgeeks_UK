Records Destruction

Clear out old records responsibly.

When paper no longer needs to occupy your office, Document Geeks can help with removal, destruction and optional digitization before the originals are destroyed.

From paper burden to clean workspace.

We can arrange pickup of records from your location and route them for destruction and recycling. If you want to retain an electronic copy, digitization can be added before destruction.

Because retention and destruction requirements depend on the type of records and your organization, confirm your own retention obligations before authorizing destruction.

Optional before-destruction scan: retain a digital image of records that you may need to reference later.
